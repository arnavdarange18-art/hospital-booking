How booking works

  1. 1

    Find a doctor

    Search by name, specialization or department, and see each doctor's next free time.

  2. 2

    Choose a time

    Open times are listed by day. A time can only be booked once, so what you see as free is free.

  3. 3

    Get confirmed

    Your booking appears under My appointments and in your notifications. Reschedule or cancel from there.

For doctors

  • Set weekly working hours and the length of each visit.
  • See each day as a timeline of booked and open slots.
  • Mark visits completed, or cancel them and free the slot.

For administrators

  • Create doctor accounts with their specialization and department.
  • Deactivate accounts that should no longer sign in.
  • Keep an eye on patients, doctors and upcoming appointments.
